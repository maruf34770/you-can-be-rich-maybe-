# You Can Be Rich (Maybe) — Multiplayer Board Game

A modern, fast-paced property trading board game inspired by **Richup.io**. Features smooth arcade animations, responsive board scaling, dynamic Web Audio sound effects, intelligent AI bots, and full support for **2 to 24 players**.

---

## 🌟 Key Features & Updates

### 1. Official Game Branding
- Titled **You Can Be Rich (Maybe)** with custom dark/neon styling and badges.

### 2. Fair Property Purchasing & Tile Inspection
- **Strict Landing Rule**: You can **only** buy the property you actually land on during your turn!
- **Read-Only Board Inspection**: Clicking on any tile on the board opens a deed details card showing its name, color group, owner avatar, rent table, house/hotel levels, and mortgage status without offering illegal purchase buttons.

### 3. Back & Close Buttons for Every Popup
- Every popup modal includes a clean Back/Close (`×`) button:
  - **Buy Modal**: Decline purchase and seamlessly send property to live auction.
  - **Live Auction Modal**: Fold from bidding or close safely.
  - **Trade Modal**: Back out of negotiations without affecting turn.
  - **Property Manager**: Close without modifying properties.
  - **Surprise & Treasure Cards**: OK and Back buttons to resume turn.
  - **Jail Choice Modal**: Close to roll or choose fine/card.
  - **Property Info Modal**: Close anytime.

### 4. 2 to 24 Player Support
- Fully configurable in the game lobby from **2 up to 24 players**.
- Mix and match **Humans** (local pass-and-play) and **AI Bots**.
- 24 unique, vibrant color presets and avatar icons (🚀, 👑, 💎, 🏎️, 🐱, 🐶, 👻, 🔥, ⭐, 🤖, etc.).
- Micro-grid token positioning on spaces prevents clutter.

### 5. Smooth Richup.io-Style Animations
- **Tile-by-Tile Hopping**: Tokens hop sequentially space by space with a bouncing jump arc and sound pops.
- **3D Animated Tumbling Dice**: Physics-style tumbling cubes in the center stage roll and settle cleanly on randomized pip layouts.
- **Floating Cash Notifications**: Animated `+$200` (green) and `-$100` (red) floaters rising above player cards and board tiles during transactions.

### 6. Full Game Mechanics
- **40-Tile Board**: 22 international cities across 8 color groups + 4 Airports + 2 Utilities.
- **Live Property Auctions**: Live auction room with a ticking timer, quick bid buttons (+ $10, + $50, + $100), and active bot bidding.
- **Interactive Trading**: Propose trades (properties + cash) with opponents; AI evaluates trades fairly.
- **Houses & Hotels**: Build up to 4 houses and upgrade to a Hotel once you own an entire color group.
- **Mortgage & Unmortgage**: Manage properties at any time to raise emergency cash.
- **Jail Mechanics**: Escape by paying $50, using a "Get Out of Jail Free" card, or rolling doubles (up to 3 attempts).
- **Free Rest (Jackpot Pool)**: Optional house rule to collect all taxes accumulated in the Free Rest zone.
- **Web Audio API**: 100% self-contained synthesized sounds with no external asset requirements.
